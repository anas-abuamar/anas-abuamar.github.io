Hi, I'm Anas.

I train world models for rare events in the ocean: right now paralytic shellfish toxin in the Gulf of Maine, at Bigelow Laboratory, where a closure turns up in roughly four percent of forecasts and a model can score beautifully while being useless. The rest of what I do asks the same question from a different side: reading behaviour in Arabic, a palliative care platform, multi-agent systems for Earth observation, all of them turning on who gets to know, and who answers when the model is confidently wrong.

Before Colby I was in Bethlehem, and then at UWC-USA in New Mexico. Somewhere in there I co-founded a competitive programming club, spent two summers modelling coral reefs on a supercomputer in Albuquerque, and learned to lead people through fourteen days in the backcountry. There's a longer list.
