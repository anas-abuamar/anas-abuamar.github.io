Eltynien

FIRST Robotics team 9620

  • team 9620 on The Blue Alliance

Eltynien is Arabic for a dragon with boundless power. It was UWC-USA’s first robotics club, and for a while it existed only as a set of virtual meetings I ran in the summer of 2023, before any of us had arrived on campus, with three students who already knew something about robots.

We laid the groundwork by designing outreach, chasing sponsors, and prototyping early robot designs in dorm rooms. By the CAS fair we had enough to show that other people wanted in. The team ended up at twenty-five, split across programming, engineering and outreach.

The parts I worked on are the ones I still care about: swerve drivetrains, PID feedback, odometry, vision-aided aiming. The part I did not expect to care about was the money. We raised ten thousand dollars in grants, and the exercise of writing those applications is what made the shape of the problem obvious to me. Robotics is expensive. Access to it is not distributed by talent.

In VEX we came through four qualifying rounds in Albuquerque to reach the World Championship in Dallas. In our rookie FIRST Lego League season the team took the Rocky All-Star award, which goes to the team that best embodies the programme’s values rather than the one that wins.
