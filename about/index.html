About

I grew up in Bethlehem and now study computer science and computational biology at Colby College in Maine. In between there was the Armand Hammer United World College in Montezuma, New Mexico, two years that account for most of the things below.

What I work on has a shape I only noticed in retrospect. The first system I met that decided who got through and explained itself to no one was a checkpoint. I now spend my time on systems that have to admit what they don't know: a forecast that has to say how sure it is, a model whose reasoning someone can actually check. The essays argue about that directly; the research tries to build it.

Outside that: the equestrian team, the ski and climbing clubs, running and biking. Most of what I know about working with people I learned somewhere with bad weather and no phone signal, and I have kept going back on purpose.

Before Colby

  • 2025Breeze of Act to Rise Our Smiles

    A summer camp in Bethlehem for children living with chronic conditions, built to reduce anxiety, to develop emotional intelligence and resilience, and to encourage the habits that make living with a chronic condition easier. It served 120 participants. I wrote about designing it.

  • 2024–25Los Jardines Institute

    Social and Racial Justice Coordinator intern in Albuquerque. I worked on their website, since a place doing this work has to be findable, and on the farm alongside the Isleta Pueblo community. The summer that became The Soil's Whisper.

  • 2023–24Eltynien, FIRST Robotics team 9620

    Founded UWC-USA's first robotics team. 25 students, $10,000 raised, and the parts I care about: swerve drivetrain, odometry, PID feedback, vision-aided aiming.

  • 2023-12Plato's Palestinian Apology

    A play drawn from lived Palestinian experience, written and performed for the UWC-USA Cultural Showcase. Two displaced siblings question their grandfather about the symbols they have inherited, about how Palestine is remembered and put back together in the present, and about what sumud, the steadfastness those symbols stand for, asks of them.The recording is here.

  • 2020–24Bethlehem competitive programming club

    Co-founded it and taught workshops with the Palestinian Ministry of Education and the Palestinian Olympiad in Informatics. Teams I coached placed 3rd at IEEExtreme and 1st in the national contest.

  • 2020–23Student council president

    Representing 8,300 students across 23 schools.
