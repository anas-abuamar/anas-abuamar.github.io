Living Together

2023

Regeneron ISEF 2023 · Third Award, Behavioral and Social Sciences

  • project record, Society for Science
  • full abstract

Getting to palliative care in the West Bank costs a day: the travel, the wait at the facility, the wait at the checkpoints. The care existed. Reaching it took more than it gave back, which is a logistics problem sitting on top of a medical one. I know the shape of that day from the other end of it, at home, waiting.

The people I was reading about carried a second problem underneath the condition. Devices they could not afford, rehabilitation centres too far to reach weekly, and a care system that treated the clinical part as the whole of it. The WHO’s 2021 World Report on Hearing counts about 1.5 billion people living with some degree of hearing loss, roughly 430 million of whom need rehabilitation.

So I built what I could build. From 2020, on a borrowed laptop, I trained a speech recognition system on audio I collected myself, sampled directly from students and from people living with hearing loss instead of from an off-the-shelf corpus. Speech models were not yet something you called an API for, and the recordings that mattered, Arabic, accented, non-standard, were not in anyone’s dataset.

Around it went an integrated care platform. A web application makes the handoff between caregiver and care recipient procedural instead of improvised. A mobile application carries an extended reality environment that teaches speaking methods through 3D scenes, which is a cheap way to practise something otherwise expensive to practise at all.

The study was not only software: interviews and site visits at rehabilitation centres and hospitals providing palliative care, then analysis of 156 patient files alongside user questionnaires. Of the respondents, 97% found the platform beneficial, 90% found it usable, 88% said it improved the work of professionals. Those are self-reported and the questionnaire sample was small. The file analysis is the sturdier half.

It took a Third Award in Behavioral and Social Sciences at the 2023 Regeneron International Science and Engineering Fair in Dallas, representing Palestine out of Bethlehem Secondary School for Boys, among 1,638 finalists from 49 states and 64 countries, regions and territories.
